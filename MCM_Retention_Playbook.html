Approval Level Key

Pre-Approved No escalation required – agent can proceed immediately
CS TL MCM Customer Success Team Lead sign-off required
RL Retention Lead sign-off required
FBP Financial Business Partner sign-off required

Sequencing rule: All save attempts must be offered in sequence. Move to the next attempt only when the previous offer has been declined.

Key Rules & Guardrails

  • FOC Cap: FOC giveaway must not exceed 1/3 of customer ARR
  • FOC Request Form: Always complete before committing PS/consultancy time
  • CRM Logging: All save attempts must be logged in CRM with outcome recorded
  • Discount Timing: Discounts apply on recontract only unless stated otherwise
  • Competitor Matching: Requires a written quote from the competitor
  • Approval in Writing: Any offer above Pre-Approved must be confirmed in writing before communicating to customer
  • Mis-selling: Must be escalated to FBP immediately – no offers without sign-off
  • Debt Recovery: Finance team must be involved from the outset
